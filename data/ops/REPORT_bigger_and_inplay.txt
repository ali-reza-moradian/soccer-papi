5. T1 — VENUE CASH, MEASURED TODAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Kalshi      $4,869.29    4.03 full-size pairs fundable (margin x1.15)   OK
  Polymarket  $2,446.84    2.03 full-size pairs fundable (margin x1.15)   OK
  TOTAL       $7,316.13

POLYMARKET IS THE TIGHT SIDE: it funds just TWO max-size pairs. At the old $525
pair it funded four. This is a real new constraint at these sizes and it is why
the check exists — the stake pools bound what we intend to spend and know nothing
about what is in each bank, and the two legs of a pair draw on DIFFERENT banks.

HOW IT WORKS: free cash is read on the existing 5-minute off-loop reconcile and
cached — never on the quoting path (a balance read there is exactly the hot-path
venue call phase 2 removed). A quote whose leg exceeds its bank's cached cash x
1.15 is refused with binding reason `venue_cash`, counted in binding_counts and
in the funnel. When either bank drops below one max pair it alerts, throttled to
once per venue per hour.
UNKNOWN IS NOT A REFUSAL: a failed read caches None and the check abstains. The
stake pools are still in force underneath, and refusing every quote because a
balance endpoint blipped would be a self-inflicted outage.

6. T2 — HEDGE-MISS INSTRUMENTATION (measurement only, no behaviour change)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE QUESTION: in-play's entire -$6.69 lifetime came from hedge MISSES, not from
bad pairs (-$2.20, -$4.75, -$0.96), and in-play allows the hedge 1500ms against
pre-game's 3000ms in faster books. Nothing recorded how long a hedge actually
took, so "is the timeout guilty?" was unanswerable.

THREE NEW CSV COLUMNS on every fill outcome row, both phases:
  hedge_ms       wall time from hedge submit to venue answer
  hedge_outcome  locked | partial | missed | error
  hedge_depth    shares the pre-hedge walk could cover at that moment

Plus a per-phase roll-up in the panel (`live.hedge_stats`): n, mean/max ms, the
phase's own timeout, outcome counts, and the last 10 MISS latencies. Every miss
also logs a line naming the elapsed ms against the phase timeout and the walked
depth.

HOW TO READ IT WHEN THE DATA ARRIVES:
  misses clustered just under 1500ms  -> the timeout is causing them (next prompt)
  misses on shallow walked depth, or at latencies well inside the timeout
                                      -> the book ran away; the timeout is innocent
NO DATA YET: zero fills since the 17:38Z deploy, so `hedge_stats` is empty. This
answers nothing today; it makes the question answerable from the next fill on.

NOTE ON SCALE, since it changes the stakes: unwind tolls scale with size. The
-$4.75 in-play exit of 2026-08-02 is ~-$9.50 at these caps, so the -$40 in-play
sub-cap is roughly FOUR bad exits, not eight.
