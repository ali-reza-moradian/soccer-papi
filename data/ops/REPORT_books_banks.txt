    not a second charge. Counting it twice would have double-booked $9.38.

T2 — EXIT COSTS NOW ENTER LIFETIME
================================================================================
Bug: an unwound market settles with us FLAT, so no trade_settled row is ever
written for it, and the toll lived only in unwind_cost_today — which rolls to
zero at UTC midnight.

New counter settled_pnl_exits_lifetime. A VERIFIED exit books into BOTH
settled_pnl_lifetime (so lifetime tracks venue cash) AND the exits bucket.
  hedge_unwound   -> always (a zero-cost row simply adds nothing)
  hedge_declined  -> only when it carries a real paid cost
  unwind_FAILED   -> NEVER: that position still exists and settles later via
                     mark_corrected/trade_settled. Booking it here double-counts.

ONE formula, one place — state.hedged_lifetime():
    hedged = lifetime - untracked - exits        (exits are negative)
used by state.summary, state.heartbeat, balance.books_from and balance.run_cli.

Idempotency: an exit persists immediately (like hedge_locked) and nothing replays
the CSV at startup. Restatement key `exits-20260804` (-6.99, n=3) is written into
the tuning file by the SAME atomic write that moves the money, and is refused
forever after. Pre-Jul-30 history untouched.
