--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THE SHORT VERSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A 115-share rest-kalshi fill hedged only 80/115 on Polymarket. The executor
computed the 35-share naked remainder CORRECTLY. Two things then went wrong:

  1. Nothing registered the 80 hedged shares. The partial branch committed the
     stake, advanced hedged_seen and went straight to the unwind — it never
     reached _record_hedge_locked, which is the ONLY path that writes the
     expected-position registry, the settled-pnl cost basis and the watch-set.
  2. The unwind then sold the hedged leg too. _verified_unwind_kalshi asked for
     35, got them, re-read the position, saw the 80 contracts we were holding ON
     PURPOSE, called that an unsold remainder and sold those as well.

Result: the Kalshi leg was liquidated, its Poly counterpart was left naked and
directional, and the ledger recorded a tidy $4.00 unwind. The 80 Under shares
were in NO registry, so orphan detection, the 5-minute reconcile and the
maker-position line of the 8-hourly audit were all blind to them BY
CONSTRUCTION — they only ever look at instruments something registered.

The match finished Under. The naked position paid $80.00 against a $54.48 cost.
That is LUCK, not edge: had the pair been booked as a pair it would have settled
at about -$0.08.

WHERE THE 80 SHARES WERE LOST, IN CODE
  src/genz/maker_rt/pregame_exec.py, _hedge_fill, the MISS/PARTIAL/ERROR branch:

      hedged = min(hedged, matched)
      if hedged > 0:
          self.caps.commit_stake(hedged * float(hedge_avg or 0.0))
          lo.hedged_seen += hedged
      remainder = max(0.0, matched - hedged)
      return self._unwind_and_record(lo, remainder, ...)

  The stake was committed and the attribution advanced, but _note_expected_legs,
  _note_pair_legs and _track_rested — all of which live inside
  _record_hedge_locked — were never called. The share COUNT was never wrong:
  PolyExec._normalize read takingAmount=80 correctly off venue cash (verified in
  tests/test_poly_hedge_sizing.py::test_the_klamci_hedge_response_reads_as_eighty_shares).
  The 80 shares were lost by the REGISTRY, not by the parser. Those two failures
  must not be conflated — the parser bug was TBTOR, 2026-07-23, and it is fixed.

  And src/genz/maker_rt/pregame_exec.py, _verified_unwind_kalshi: the
  reconcile-to-flat loop's target was ZERO. Its stop condition asked "is the
  venue position empty?" when the right question is "is there anything beyond
  what we hold on purpose?".

WHY LARIBE WAS FINE — AND WHAT IT ACTUALLY WAS
  The prompt describes LARIBE as a hedge that filled fully. Venue truth says
  otherwise, and the correction sharpens the diagnosis rather than weakening it:
  LARIBE (KXUELTOTAL-26AUG06LARIBE-6, 17:38:28Z) was a rest-POLY fill of 42

A SECOND, LATENT INSTANCE OF THE SAME UNWIND BUG
  The keep-floor failure needs no partial hedge at all. On any multi-fill order:
  fill #1 hedges in full and registers 60 expected contracts; fill #2 misses and
  asks to unwind 40; the loop sells the 40, re-reads 60, and liquidates fill #1's
  properly hedged rest leg. Pinned by
  test_a_second_fills_unwind_does_not_liquidate_the_first_fills_hedge.

3. WHAT CHANGED (T3, T4, T5 + the side fix)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
T3  A PARTIAL HEDGE IS A SMALLER PAIR, NOT A FAILURE
    * _hedge_fill: on a shortfall the FILLED portion is now booked through
      _record_partial_pair -> _record_hedge_locked. Both legs land in the
      expected-position registry, the cost basis reaches the settlement
      reconciler, the watch-set gets the instrument, and the locked net is
      computed from the ACTUAL hedge fill price and fee. Only the shortfall is
      unwound. One venue execution still consumes exactly ONE of the day's fill
      slots (fills_today is a cap; the unwind half books its pnl through
      adjust_pnl, the established "restate without counting a fill" doctrine).
    * LiveHedger.hedge / .hedge_poly now report hedge_fee on the PARTIAL branch
      too — a partial bought real shares at a real fee, and without it the
      sub-pair's locked net is one fee optimistic.
    * _verified_unwind{,_kalshi,_poly}: "flat" now means "nothing beyond what we
      hold on purpose". The sell floor and the stop condition come from
      _expected_shares(rest_venue, token), and `remaining` is reported NET of it
      so a caller's orphan check sees only genuinely naked shares.
    * Share-count plumbing: verified already correct and PINNED, not changed.
      _normalize takes the SIDE's share leg (BUY -> takingAmount) and the
      executed price from the making/taking RATIO. New property tests cover FAK
      fills at 0/30/70/100%, plus the literal KLAMCI response.

T4  OUR OWN EXITS RECOGNISE THEMSELVES
    * _kalshi_unwind_sell registers the instrument as "unwinding" BEFORE the sell
      is submitted (there is no order id yet, and the fill sweep can route an
      execution the instant it fills), then adds the returned order id to
      _own_order_ids. _untracked_fill labels those unwind_confirmed instead of
      fill_untracked, and never orphans on them. The marker expires after 300s so
      it can never explain away a real surprise an hour later.
    * The hedge_unwound row now records the size ACTUALLY SOLD, not the size
      requested. The cost was always computed on the true total; only the label
      was wrong, and the label is what a human reconstructs an incident from.

T5  THE BLIND SPOT, CLOSED
    * New 48h QUOTED scope (data/ops/maker_rt_quoted_markets.json): every market
      we offered on, BOTH legs — the one we rest and the complement we would
      hedge into. Written at placement, pruned at 48h, persisted across restarts.
    * _sweep_unregistered runs inside the 5-minute reconcile (reads batched
      off-loop with everything else, so the loop does not block). For any
      instrument in that scope that is in NO registry — not the watch-set, not
      the expected legs, not the provisional marks, not an open order — and whose
      venue position exceeds 0.5 shares, it fires a RED Telegram naming the
      market, writes a position_unregistered ledger row, and logs CRITICAL.
    * It ALERTS, it does not halt. An unregistered position is a bookkeeping
      failure whose right response is a human looking at it; halting the maker
      over a position that may well be perfectly hedged trades a silent failure
      for a loud useless one. Throttled to one scream per instrument per hour.
    * Scoped to quoted markets ON PURPOSE: this funder wallet holds ~180
      positions that are not ours, and a blanket sweep would alarm on all of them.

SIDE FIX  THE DIGEST'S TWO WINDOWS NOW SAY WHICH IS WHICH
    "Currently offering 2/2 · today $594/$1200 used · 0 fills · +$1.67" put the
    INTERVAL's fill count next to TODAY's stake and pnl. On 2026-08-04 that
    printed "0 fills · +$1.67" on an eleven-fill day — either impossible or an
    admission that two counters disagree, with no way to tell which. Line 1 is
    now the interval ("42 offers placed · 0 taken"); everything after "Today:" is
    the day, including the fill count.
