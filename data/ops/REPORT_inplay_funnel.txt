--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THE SHORT VERSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In-play is not being blocked by a direction restriction or the venue minimum. It
is blocked by THREE things, in sequence — two anti-phantom rails that between them
consume 85% of evaluations, and then a budget that refuses everything that is
left:

  1. SHOCK-FREEZE blackouts eat 48.3% of in-play evaluations. A mid move of >=5%
     within 10s freezes a node for 30s. Only 25 freeze EVENTS fired in the window,
     but they blacked out 45,295 evaluations — because a live match's mid moves
     5% often, and each freeze silences the node for 30 seconds.

  2. HEDGE-THIN COOLDOWN eats a further 36.3%. Three hedge_too_thin refusals
     within 10 minutes put a node to sleep for FIFTEEN MINUTES. Pre-game hits this
     only 13.3% of the time — a 2.7x difference, and the rule was tuned on
     pre-game behaviour.

  3. Of the 0.69% of in-play evaluations that survive to the executor, ONE
     HUNDRED PERCENT are refused for size, and 99.2% of those refusals bind on
     `daily_stake`. Not the venue minimum. Not book depth. The daily budget.

The daily budget is not exhausted by SPENDING. Right now: $58.22 spent of $1,200.
It is exhausted by RESERVATIONS. Every resting quote holds its full projected
pair cost against the daily cap until it fills, cancels or ages out — and quotes
fill 0.67% of the time. 12 slots x ~$100 of typical projected pair = ~$1,200 =
exactly the daily cap. So the slots fill with pre-game, the budget is fully
reserved, headroom goes to ~$0, and every later candidate — every in-play
candidate — is sized at 0 shares and refused.

THE ROOT CAUSE IN ONE LINE: `max_open_quotes` x typical-pair-reservation is
numerically equal to `max_daily_stake_usd`, so the concurrency cap and the daily
spend cap are the same cap, and pre-game wins it every time.

AND THE THING THAT SHOULD STOP YOU RAISING ANY OF IT (section 3): across every
in-play fill this system has ever taken, realized P&L is NEGATIVE $6.69.

1.1 WHY THIS NEEDED NEW COUNTERS

Nothing in the build could answer "where do the evaluations go", for two reasons
that both had to be fixed to get an honest table:

  * QUOTE-CONDITIONAL. ``_expire_if_open`` writes an expire row only when a quote
    is already armed. A candidate that never arms produces NO row. A path that
    never quotes is therefore invisible BY CONSTRUCTION — which is exactly the
    path under investigation.
  * UNEVENLY THROTTLED. ``below_venue_minimum`` wrote a CSV row EVERY TICK;
    slot refusals wrote one row per 300s per node. In the all-time CSV that makes
    below_venue_minimum (308,010 rows, 65.8%) look ~3x more common than
    max_open_quotes (114,320 rows, 24.4%) when the throttle alone explains the
    gap. Any funnel built from those rows is wrong and cannot be corrected after
    the fact.

So the funnel below comes from new counters: one increment per evaluation, at the
branch that consumed it, keyed (sport, phase, stage). They are pure
instrumentation, guarded at both call sites, and pinned by tests that assert a
placement still places and a refusal still refuses.

2. PART 2 — IS THE EDGE REAL AT OUR SIZES?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
YES, on the hedge-depth question. The hedge book is not the problem, and it is
not close.

Method: every in-play sizing refusal logs the live hedge depth at the ask and the
rest-book depth at the moment it refused. n = 345,518 such moments, all-time.

   hedge depth at the ask   p10 = 2,707    p50 = 18,371   p90 = 303,798  shares
   rest book depth          p10 =     5    p50 =  8,727   p90 = 1,903,758

   hedge depth <   1 share :       0   (0.00%)
   hedge depth <   5 shares:       0   (0.00%)
   hedge depth <  25 shares:       0   (0.00%)
   hedge depth < 100 shares:       0   (0.00%)
   hedge depth < 500 shares:   3,892   (1.13%)

Our venue minimum is 5 shares on Polymarket (or ceil($1/price), so 25 shares at
4c) and 1 contract on Kalshi. In 345,518 in-play moments where we wanted to quote
and could not, the hedge book NEVER had less than 100 shares at the ask. The
median had 18,371.

SO THE ANSWER TO "is in-play edge only real below the venue minimum" IS NO. The
edge was executable at our minimum size essentially always, and at far more than
our minimum size in the median case. The gap between "edge existed" and "edge was
executable" is not a market-liquidity gap at all — it is entirely our own budget:

   the binding limiter on in-play sizing refusals   daily_stake  99.19%
                                                    book_depth    0.81%

WHAT I COULD NOT MEASURE HERE, AND WHY: I could not tie hedge depth to the
specific achievable samples that read >=0.5%. The achievable ladder is a reservoir
of NET VALUES only — it does not retain depth, and the CSV `achievable_sample`
row (throttled to 1/min/market) carries no depth column either. The depth
distribution above is taken over the refusal moments instead, which is a superset
of, not identical to, the >=0.5% moments. It is strong evidence that depth is
never the binding constraint in-play; it is not a per-sample join. Adding a depth
field to the achievable sampler would close this properly.

4. PART 4 — WHAT WOULD IT TAKE (PROPOSALS ONLY — NOTHING IMPLEMENTED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ranked by (expected quotes unlocked) / (risk added). Every estimate names its
evidence. I have implemented NONE of these.

READ SECTION 3.2 FIRST. In-play's entire realized record is -$6.69 across 12
fills. Everything below buys VOLUME in a phase that has never yet made money, and
the achievable ladder that says in-play has 4x the edge is a MODEL of joining the
best bid — it cannot see adverse selection, which is the one thing in-play has
more of. I have flagged which proposals trade safety for volume.

--- P1. SEPARATE THE CONCURRENCY CAP FROM THE DAILY SPEND CAP ------------------
    THE ROOT CAUSE. A resting quote reserves its full projected pair against
    `max_daily_stake_usd` for its whole life, and quotes fill 0.67% of the time.
    12 slots x ~$100 typical projected pair = ~$1,200 = the entire daily cap, so
    the budget is ~150x over-reserved against expected consumption and the cap
    that is supposed to bound the DAY's spend is actually bounding CONCURRENCY.
    PROPOSAL: give outstanding reservations their own ceiling (a concurrent-
    exposure cap) and let `max_daily_stake_usd` bound only money actually
    committed (fills + hedges + unwinds), which is what its name and its
    docstring already say it is.
    EXPECTED: unblocks 100% of the 330-per-167s in-play executor arrivals that
    are currently refused, and 68% of pre-game's. Historical placement rate when
    headroom existed: 150-180 in-play quotes/day (20260730: 180, 20260802: 174,
    20260722: 157).
    RISK: MODERATE, AND THIS ONE TRADES SAFETY FOR VOLUME. The reservation is
    what makes the daily cap a hard PRE-commitment; without it the cap becomes
    soft, and a correlated burst of fills could overshoot the day's budget before
    anything stops it. Mitigation: size the concurrent-exposure cap so that
    worst-case (every resting quote fills at once) still lands inside the daily
    cap — that is a real constraint, just a smaller one than 12 x $525.
    EVIDENCE: live counters (330/330 in-play executor arrivals refused,
    limiter=daily_stake 99.19% of 345,518 all-time refusals); live state at
    16:02Z showing $58.22 spent of $1,200 with headroom ~$0.

--- P2. SHORTEN THE TWO IN-PLAY BLACKOUTS ---------------------------------------
    (a) SHOCK-FREEZE, 48.3% of in-play evaluations. `shock_move: 0.05` /
        `shock_window_s: 10` / `freeze_s: 30`: one 5% mid move silences a node for
        30s, and 25 freeze events blacked out 45,295 evaluations in 343s. Options:
        raise `shock_move` (5% is small for a live match — a single goal moves a
        totals mid far more than that), or cut `freeze_s`, or scale the freeze to
        the size of the move instead of a flat 30s.
    (b) HEDGE-THIN COOLDOWN, 36.3%. 3 hedge_too_thin refusals within 10 minutes
        sleep a node for 15 MINUTES. In-play hits it 36.3% vs pre-game's 13.3%. A
        15-minute blackout is enormous relative to a live market's life, and the
        rule was tuned on pre-game behaviour. Options: make it phase-aware
        (in-play 15min -> 2-3min) or require more refusals (3 -> 6).
    Do NOT remove either. The freeze is the anti-phantom rail that stops us
    quoting into a book that just gapped — which is exactly when adverse selection
    is worst — and the cooldown stops arm-then-cancel churn. Both are real.
    PROPOSAL: make the cooldown phase-aware — e.g. in-play 15min -> 2-3min, or
    require more refusals (3 -> 6) before it trips. Do NOT remove it; it exists
    to stop arm-then-cancel churn, which is real.
    EXPECTED: together these gate ~85% of in-play evaluations, so relaxing them
    is the difference between a trickle and a stream reaching the pricing stage.
    BUT ON THEIR OWN THEY BUY ZERO EXTRA QUOTES — everything recovered would then
    die at daily_stake, exactly as the 646 that already get there do. Worth doing
    ONLY AFTER P1, and the true yield cannot be estimated until P1 lands.
    RISK: THIS IS THE ONE THAT TRADES SAFETY FOR VOLUME, and it is the most
    dangerous proposal here. Both rails exist to keep us OUT of the book at the
    moments adverse selection is worst — a node that just gapped 5%, or one whose
    hedge keeps evaporating. Section 3.2 is the warning: in-play's losses came
    from hedge MISSES (-$2.20, -$4.75, -$0.96), not from bad pairs. Relaxing (b)
    specifically re-admits the nodes whose hedge book was already proven
    unreliable. If any proposal here reproduces those exits, it is this one.

--- P3. GIVE IN-PLAY A RESERVED SHARE OF SLOTS AND BUDGET ----------------------
    `reserve_per_direction: 3` guarantees slots per DIRECTION but nothing
    guarantees them per PHASE, so pre-game (118 markets, ~93k evals/167s) beats
    in-play (~32k evals/167s) to every slot and every dollar. At 16:02Z: 11 of 12
    slots pre-game, 0 in-play.
    PROPOSAL: a per-phase reservation mirroring the per-direction one — e.g.
    guarantee in-play 2-3 of 12 slots and a slice of the exposure cap.
    EXPECTED: bounded and predictable — 2-3 concurrent in-play quotes, which at
    the observed in-play quote lifetime is on the order of 50-150 quotes/day.
    RISK: LOW on its own; it REDISTRIBUTES exposure rather than adding it. But it
    deliberately moves capital from a phase with 47 fills into a phase with 12
    fills and -$6.69 realized. That is a strategy decision, not a plumbing one.

--- P4. LEAVE THE VENUE-MINIMUM FLOOR EXACTLY WHERE IT IS ----------------------
    NOT A CONSTRAINT. Measured, not assumed: of in-play sizing refusals, 99.19%
    bind on daily_stake and 0.81% on book_depth — `venue_minimum` binds on 0%.
    Among quotes that WERE successfully sized, venue_minimum was the binding
    constraint 88 times pre-game and 0 times in-play. Lowering it would change
    nothing and would only reduce size on quotes that already place.
    EXPECTED EXTRA QUOTES: ZERO. Recommend NO CHANGE.

--- P5. LEAVE THE IN-PLAY CIRCUIT ALONE (FOR NOW) ------------------------------
    `first_fill_pause_s: 120` fired 7 times ever; on a day with 1-2 in-play fills
    that is ~0.1% of the day. `halt_locked_net: -0.020` fired twice, both times
    on genuinely bad fills (-2.65% after a $4.75 unwind). Neither is throttling
    in-play "before it starts" — in-play is dying 1,000x earlier in the funnel,
    at cooldown and budget.
    EXPECTED EXTRA QUOTES from relaxing either: NEGLIGIBLE.
    RECOMMENDATION: no change, and specifically do not relax `halt_locked_net`.
    It is the only thing that has ever stopped an in-play losing streak, and
    in-play's realized record is negative.

--- P6. NOTHING TO DO ABOUT DIRECTIONS -----------------------------------------
    Both rest directions are already live in-play (575 rest-kalshi / 375
    rest-poly orders placed). The config comment saying otherwise was wrong and
    is corrected in this commit. No behaviour change proposed.

SEQUENCING. P1 is the only one that unblocks anything by itself; P2 and P3 are
multipliers on it and are worth nothing before it. If the goal is to learn
whether in-play edge is real, the cheapest honest experiment is P3 alone — a
small, ring-fenced in-play allocation — because it bounds the downside to a known
slice of capital while producing the fills that section 3.2 shows we do not yet
have enough of to judge.

5. WHAT I COULD NOT MEASURE, AND WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Per-sample join of hedge depth to the >=0.5% achievable moments (section 2).
     The ladder keeps net values only and the throttled CSV sample has no depth
     column. Would need a depth field on the achievable sampler.
  2. The `expire_before_kickoff_s` 120s gap and `horizon_hours` were asked for as
     funnel stages. The gap window is measured (`gap_window` counter, 0 in this
     window because no candidate was inside it) but `horizon_hours` filters the
     UNIVERSE upstream of the driver, so a market outside it never becomes a
     candidate and cannot be counted at this layer at all. Measuring it needs a
     counter in the universe builder.
  3. `conn_fresh_s` / `node_quiet_max_s` / `stale_grace_s` are now countable
     (`rails_gated_not_fresh`, `inplay_stale`) but read 0 in this window — the
     sockets were healthy throughout. They are not exonerated, just not exercised
     yet; a longer window will say.
  4. The 24h funnel the prompt asked for is a 343-SECOND funnel. The counters did
     not exist until 15:59:43Z today. The TOP of the funnel has not converged —
     the #1 and #2 stages swapped between the 32k-eval and 94k-eval readings
     (cooldown 53.8%/frozen 21.3% -> frozen 48.3%/cooldown 36.3%), so treat the
     ORDER of those two as unsettled. The BOTTOM is stable across every reading
     (~0.7-1.0% reach the executor, 100% refused on daily_stake, 0 placed) and
     that is what the conclusions rest on. No absolute daily counts are presented
     as measurements. A genuine 24h table exists tomorrow.
  5. Whether the 2026-08-04 caps raise CAUSED the August collapse in in-play
     quoting (section 3.1). The correlation is clean and the mechanism is
     arithmetic, but I did not isolate it — no A/B, and other things changed.
