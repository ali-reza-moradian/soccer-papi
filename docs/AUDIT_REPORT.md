## 1. Executive summary

1. **The books are currently fiction, and the risk rails were silently disabled today.** Kalshi v2 returns fill prices in YES-space; the write path converts NO orders out (`kalshi_exec.py:302`) but the read path never converts back (`:333-353`). Every Kalshi **NO-side** hedge — i.e. every soccer-totals hedge — books at `1 − real price`. It fired 3× on Jul 29: FORBOT booked **+$320.05** (real ≈ +$2.35), TOTSYD **+$63.14** (real ≈ +$1–3), CERBVB **−$49.91** phantom loss (real ≈ −$0.23). `pnl_today` is +$330 of fiction → the $50 daily-loss halt needed ~**−$380 real** to trip; real cash staked ≈ **$1,069 vs the $800 cap** while books read $740.
2. **True hedged-maker lifetime edge is ≈ $0** (corrected: between −$1.18 and +$1.17). Essentially all reported profit is one untracked naked-leg windfall (+$42 UFC ghost). The booked +$53.63 headline and hedged +$10.16 are contaminated.
3. Three more independently confirmed P0s: the UTC-midnight roll **clears a latched orphan halt** (happened twice in production — the bot resumed quoting over unverified naked positions); the Kalshi fill sweep **adds** fill counts with no shared dedupe across three detectors (first partial Kalshi fill with a *successful* hedge will **double-hedge real money**); hedge accounting conflates **cumulative venue position with per-fill delta** (can skip unwinding genuinely naked shares).
4. The in-play −2% day-halt, the 120s first-fill pause, and the closed-market skip list live only in process memory — with 11–21 restarts/day routine (gitguard restarts on every commit).
5. Speed: the soccer cycle (438–770s) is 85% papermaker, and that 85% is a **redundant serial re-download of order books pricing already fetched** — a no-decoupling in-process fix drops the cycle to ~65–110s. The maker loop freezes 1.6–4.5s every 10s on a serial sync fill poll (p99 stall 4.6s, max 13.7s vs a 250ms budget).
6. Markets: capacity binds hard (117,128 slot-refusal rows on Jul 29 by 12:40Z — 99.3% soccer) but a slot raise alone is inert: $800/day ≈ 2.3 max-size pairs, there is no per-game concentration cap, and the $800 "stake cap" is a placement throttle, not an exposure bound (12 open × $350 = $4,200 committable).
7. **Do not re-arm live until** the price-space decode is fixed, Jul-29 books are reconciled against venue statements, and the orphan/in-play halt persistence holes are closed. Do not raise any cap on today's booked numbers.
